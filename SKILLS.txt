MongoDB MCP (optional, for live Atlas inspection)
  1. Copy .cursor/mcp.json.example → .cursor/mcp.json
  2. Paste MONGODB_URI from server/.env as MDB_MCP_CONNECTION_STRING
  3. Or run: npx mongodb-mcp-server@latest setup
  4. Restart Cursor

Refresh MongoDB skills from upstream:
  curl -L https://github.com/mongodb/agent-skills/archive/main.tar.gz | tar xz
  cp -R agent-skills-main/skills/* .cursor/skills/
  (preserve Amelia partner skills — do not overwrite elevenlabs-amelia etc.)
